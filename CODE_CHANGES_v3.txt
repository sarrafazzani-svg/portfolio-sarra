================================================================================

TECHNICAL EXPLANATION
================================================================================

WHY THESE CHANGES WORK:

1. BIGGER CIRCLES:
   • Tablet: 75vw height creates square-ish gallery (95vw wide × 75vw tall)
   • Mobile: 85vw height creates square gallery (100vw wide × 85vw tall)
   • Square shapes take up more vertical space = bigger circles

2. SMALLER CARDS:
   • Tablet: Reduced to 90-110 × 110-135px (was 120-140 × 150-175px)
   • Mobile: Reduced to 75-100 × 95-125px (was 100-130 × 130-160px)
   • Smaller cards fit in bigger circle without overlapping

3. NO OVERLAPPING:
   • Arc radius is proportional to circle size
   • Cards are positioned around arc at consistent radius
   • Smaller cards = more space between them
   • Result: Clean, spacious circular loop

4. ASPECT RATIO CHANGE:
   • Desktop: 2:1 (landscape) - good for wide monitors
   • Tablet: auto (allows 95×75 = ~1.27:1, square-ish)
   • Mobile: auto (allows 100×85 = ~1.18:1, almost square)
   • Square-ish shapes maximize screen usage on smaller devices
